Find the iris.
Then prove it.

IrisVision localizes the human iris in real time from a webcam or photo, fits a sub-pixel circle to it, and then independently validates that fit with classical computer vision — Hough-gradient circle detection and radial intensity contrast — before reporting a verdict.

Pipeline

From pixels to a validated iris

Every frame flows through six stages. A learned model proposes; classical vision verifies.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Frames come from getUserMedia or a local image. Nothing is uploaded — all compute runs in WebAssembly and WebGL.

  2. 02

    Face landmarks

    MediaPipe Face Landmarker regresses 478 3D landmarks, including 5 points per iris (center + 4 limbus points).

  3. 03

    Circle fit

    A centered algebraic least-squares (Kåsa) fit over the limbus points gives a sub-pixel center, radius and RMS residual.

    min Σ (x² + y² + Dx + Ey + F)²
  4. 04

    Eye state

    Eye Aspect Ratio detects blinks with hysteresis; the iris position between canthi and lids yields a gaze estimate.

    EAR = (‖p₂−p₆‖ + ‖p₃−p₅‖) / 2‖p₁−p₄‖
  5. 05

    Validation

    The eye region is cropped and checked by OpenCV Hough-gradient circle detection and a radial intensity contrast test.

  6. 06

    Metrics

    Using the near-constant human iris diameter (~11.7 mm) and a pinhole model, the system estimates camera distance.

    Z = f · 11.7 mm / dpx

Validation

Why a second opinion matters

Landmark models always output an answer — even when the eye is closed, blurred or occluded. IrisVision only trusts a fit that agrees with evidence in the pixels.

Geometric agreement 45%

HoughCircles runs on a blurred, histogram-equalized grayscale crop of the eye. The strongest candidates are compared to the landmark fit by center error (in radii) and radius ratio.

g = (1 − Δc/0.6r) · (1 − |1 − rh/r|/0.5)

Radial contrast 35%

The iris is darker than the surrounding sclera. Mean intensity inside 0.7r is compared to a 1.25r–1.9r ring, sampled only inside the eyelid polygon.

k = (Iring − Iiris) / Iring

Eye openness 20%

A closed or squinting eye hides the limbus. Low EAR marks the eye as OCCLUDED rather than pretending to localize it.

o = clamp((EAR − 0.15) / 0.12)
PASS score ≥ 0.60 FAIL score < 0.60, with the weakest signal as reason OCCLUDED EAR < 0.17
